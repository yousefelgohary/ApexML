# Netflix Titles Dataset

## About the Dataset

This dataset contains information about movies and TV shows available on
Netflix, including their type, release information, ratings, duration,
genres, and descriptions.

## Columns

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Column                              Description
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  `show_id`                           Unique identifier for each movie or
                                      TV show.

  `type`                              Content type: Movie or TV Show.

  `title`                             Name of the movie or TV show.

  `director`                          Director or directors of the
                                      content.

  `cast`                              Main actors and cast members.

  `country`                           Country or countries associated
                                      with the content.

  `date_added`                        Date when the content was added to
                                      Netflix.

  `release_year`                      Year when the movie or TV show was
                                      released.

  `rating`                            Content rating, such as TV-MA,
                                      PG-13, TV-14, etc.

  `duration`                          Movie duration in minutes or number
                                      of seasons for TV shows.

  `listed_in`                         Genres or categories assigned to
                                      the content.

  `description`                       Short description or summary of the
                                      content.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## Dataset Use

The dataset can be used for Exploratory Data Analysis and Visualization
to investigate patterns in Netflix content, such as:

-   Distribution of Movies vs. TV Shows
-   Content released over the years
-   Most common genres
-   Content ratings
-   Countries producing Netflix content
-   Movie duration
-   Number of seasons for TV Shows
